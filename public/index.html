Mounting checklist

  1. Mount the device 2–4 ft above the belt, lens pointing straight down, and plug it in.
  2. Add steady lighting over the belt. Avoid glare from shop lights on glossy print.
  3. Start the camera and draw the yellow trigger zone across the belt where each product fully shows.
  4. With the belt empty, tap Learn empty belt.
  5. Take the good-sample photo from this same position.
  6. Run 10–20 products and check that each one triggers exactly once. Adjust sensitivity and photo delay if needed.
