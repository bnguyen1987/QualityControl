Set up this camera

Work through the steps in order. A step turns green when it's done. When a new order starts, only steps 2, 5 and 6 need updating.

1

Connect

Enter the plant access code. Each computer needs it once.
2

Machine and job

Shows on every alarm and in the log.
3

Camera

Pick the USB camera, start it, and aim it so a whole product fits in the picture.
  1. Choose the camera from the list, then click Start camera. Click Allow if the browser asks.
  2. Mount it 2–4 ft above the belt, pointing straight down, with steady light and no glare.
4

Trigger zone and empty belt

Tells the camera when a product has arrived.
  1. Click Set trigger zone and drag a box across the belt where each product fully shows (use the camera picture in step 3).
  2. Keep the yellow box clear for a few seconds. The camera learns the empty belt and sets itself up on the first few products. Nothing to adjust.
5

What to check

The AI only looks for what's ticked here.
6

Good sample

A photo of an approved product. The AI compares every product against it. Retake it on every job change.
